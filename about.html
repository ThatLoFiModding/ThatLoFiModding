BORN IN THE MUD, CODED IN THE STUDIO

Agriculture is more than just driving machines. It's about the smell of diesel, the timing of the harvest, and the constant battle against the elements. At ThatLoFiModding, we believe that simulation should reflect this reality.

The journey started not behind a high-end PC, but on the seat of a Deutz D4006. Working on a real farm teaches you things a video game usually ignores: the way hay changes when it gets damp, the weight of a bale that hasn't dried properly, and the frustration of losing yield to a sudden rainstorm.

Our mission is to translate these "dirty" details into clean code. We specialize in complex LUA scripts that add layers of depth to Farming Simulator, making the game feel less like a sandbox and more like a true agricultural challenge.

Every mod we release is a tribute to the hard work of real farmers. We focus on quality, performance, and stability—ensuring that our scripts run smoothly even on the largest 4x maps.
